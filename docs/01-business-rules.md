# Business rules

People sign in or create an account using a verified email and a one-time code. The first sign-in can collect a display name, and the profile can change the display name and picture. The optional seeded board contains four ordered columns and twelve initial cards. It becomes available to the account matching the configured owner email after a successful sign-in. A card has a required title, optional description, priority, tags and zero or more assignees from the same board. Assignee suggestions come from the current board and match names or initials without regard to case or accents. Selecting a person adds a removable chip. Tag suggestions come from the current board. The open card can add a tag that is missing from the suggestions. Filters select existing tags. Priorities are LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH and URGENT. Cards can be created, edited, moved or deleted. The bootstrap seed runs once for a given board.

Boards are private. An owner can create up to five boards and manage invitations and membership. Editors can create, edit, move and delete cards. Viewers can read a board and share links to its cards. A person can join up to ten boards owned by others. Invitations are tied to a verified email address and expire after seven days. The recipient accepts them from their workspace, and can leave a shared board. Removing access does not remove existing card assignments.

The sidebar is always available for switching and creating boards, managing membership, and accepting invitations. Use its own control to collapse or expand it. On narrow screens it starts collapsed and returns to that state after selecting or creating a board. In the expanded sidebar, each owned board has an ellipsis that opens board settings for renaming or deleting it. Renaming keeps its links. After confirmation, deletion permanently removes the board and its contents and ends access for all members. The profile button in the header shows the signed-in person's picture when available.

Board owners use **Add group** after the last group and use column settings to rename, recolor or delete columns in Board and List. New columns appear at the end and start gray unless the owner selects another color. The palette offers gray, red, amber, green, teal, blue, purple and pink. The starter columns keep their own colors. Names are trimmed, limited to 80 characters and cannot exactly match another column name on the same board. Changing a name or color keeps the column's cards and links. Deletion requires confirmation and an empty column. A board keeps at least one column.

Columns sort by position then ID. Cards sort by position then ID within a column. New cards append. The column opens a title field. Enter or leaving the field creates the card with medium priority and empty details. Leaving a blank title or pressing Escape discards the draft. Open the card to edit its details. Moves identify a destination column and the card to insert before. A null anchor appends. When filtering, the next visible card is the anchor and the server uses its immediate neighbor in the full list. Dropping a card at its original visible slot is a no-op. The active card is excluded from the list before computing placement.

Positions are finite double-precision numbers. Use a stride of 1024 for the first position and at either end. For interior insertion take the midpoint. When the gap is less than 0.000001 or the result is not strictly between its neighbors, assign the final destination order consecutive strides in the same transaction. Only changed cards are written, and every changed card gets a new version. A normal move writes one card plus the board revision.

Edits, moves and deletes require the card version last seen by the client. The first committed writer wins. Stale writes, deleted cards, or a destination anchor that has moved return HTTP 409 with the latest card or null. Invalid payloads return 400 and unknown boards or columns return 404.

Search filters the board by title and description without regard to case or accents. Suggestions open matching cards or replace the text query with an assignee filter. Ctrl or Command plus K focuses search outside dialogs. The assignee filter matches any card assigned to the selected person. Unassigned matches cards with an empty assignment list. Different filter dimensions combine with AND. Selected values within a dimension combine with OR. Click or press Enter to open a card. Edit the title and description in place. Text edits save when focus leaves the field, Enter saves the title, and Ctrl or Command plus Enter saves the description. Escape cancels a text edit. Priority changes and each tag or assignee addition or removal save immediately. Selecting a status in the open card moves the card to the end of that column. Drag from anywhere on the card with a mouse, or hold briefly and drag on touch. Keyboard dragging uses Space to pick up, arrow keys to navigate insertion slots and columns, Space or Enter to drop, and Escape to cancel. Announce pickup, destination, drop and cancellation, and restore focus to the moved card when possible.

Preferences apply to the current browser. Choose Small, Medium, or Large text and optionally show full descriptions in Board view. On narrow screens, the bottom toolbar holds search, filters and the view switch. Tap the search icon to reveal and focus the field. Closing search keeps the current query applied. On desktop, search stays visible and the filters dialog can replace inline filter controls.

The toolbar switches between Board and List and remembers the last view on this browser. The list groups cards by status in board order and keeps the card order within each group. Each list row uses a single line. On narrow screens it shows the title, a colored priority icon, assignees and pending sync status. Open the card to see descriptions and tags. The same filters, search, card details and direct links work in both views. People can add a card to a status group and change its status from the open card. Drag and drop is available in Board. Finish or cancel a new card draft before switching views.

**Add task** sits below the cards in each status group and starts a draft in that group. With a mouse, List reveals this action on group hover and **Add group** at the end of the list on hover. Hover over a status heading to reveal the ellipsis for column settings. Keyboard focus reveals each action, and touch layouts keep the actions visible.

Board cards and desktop list rows show as many tags as fit in the available space. A **+N** count appears when there are more tags, and hovering over it shows their names. Assignee options and chips use the profile picture of active board members when available and fall back to initials. Card previews show all assignee avatars when there are up to three people. For four or more, they show the first two people and a **+N** count for the rest.

By default, Board cards use a fixed preview height, with up to two lines for the title and one line for the description. The full-description preference allows previews to grow. Board cards show a four-segment priority bar above the title. Low fills one segment, Medium fills two, High fills three and Urgent fills all four. Hover over the bar to see the full priority label. List shows the priority name on desktop and an icon on narrow screens.

If the saved title or description changes while a person is editing it, the open card keeps their draft and shows the current saved text. They can keep the current text or explicitly apply their draft. If the field changes again before that write, the person reviews the new value before applying the draft again.

Private boards require an online session. A connection failure can leave a change available for retry while the current page is open. Sign-out, account changes and reopening the page clear in-memory private data.

The Add tag button sits before the selected tags. Tag suggestions wrap across horizontal rows in a floating panel that can extend past the open card's edge. The card has no internal scrollbar, and tall cards scroll at the viewport edge.

Opening a card from the board or search updates the address with the board and card identifiers. Existing links with a title slug still open for authorized people. A new link carries only identifiers so it does not expose the card title. Titles can repeat, and a link keeps working if the card moves to another column or its title changes. Reloading or opening that address restores the card after the board loads, even when filters hide it. Browser Back closes a card opened from the board, and Forward can reopen it. Closing a card opened directly by link keeps the person on the board. If the board confirms that a card is unavailable, show a notice and remove its link parameters. A connection failure leaves the link in place for retry.

The link icon shows **Copy link** on hover or keyboard focus. A successful copy briefly shows and announces **Copied!**. If clipboard access fails, a selected text field offers manual copying. A link opens for people with access to the board and does not grant membership.
